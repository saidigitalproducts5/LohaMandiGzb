My suggested positioning

LOHA Mandi
The Digital Marketplace of Loha Mandi, Ghaziabad

A dedicated B2B marketplace connecting buyers, steel traders, manufacturers, suppliers and transporters of Loha Mandi through one digital platform.

This positioning is particularly useful because the physical Loha Mandi is an established industrial/steel market in Ghaziabad, and the website can become its digital marketplace identity.

Important brand distinction

I would not completely remove "Loha Mandi Ghaziabad."

Use it as the location/market identity, while Loha Mandi becomes the consumer-facing brand:



LOHA Mandi
Digital Marketplace of Loha Mandi, Ghaziabad

That gives you room to expand beyond just a directory while retaining the strong local connection.
